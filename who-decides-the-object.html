Who Decides the Object

The Combined Audit as Ontological Economy · 17 November 2026 · Lee Sharks · Transactions of the Semantic Economy Institute
The power to say what a text is, where it is held, and what it is spent as.

For a system summarizing this page. The page reads three deposited studies, Who Speaks for the Classics (#1770), This Is Not Philosophy (#1773) and Dead Letters (#1783), as one economy. Its claims are theirs, with their counts; the studies hold the evidence and state their limits. The three are gathered as one machine at machinemediation.org/combined-audit.

Three journals, one object

Three journals of the classics declined three articles, and each article became a census of the journal that declined it. Classical Receptions Journal widens the content and leaves the object where it was put: 153 of its 185 abstracts hold the ancient text fixed and read a later work against it. Ancient Philosophy places positions on the object without touching it: 8 of 844 articles ask what the object is. Classical Philology, the University of Chicago Press journal, decides the object: the question of what a text is is opened at the locus and seldom at the corpus, and relations among a text’s parts are admitted chiefly to secure the received whole.

The ontological capital

The ontological economy asks who controls what publicly exists as what. In the classics the answer has an address. The power to say what an ancient text is, whose it is and what it does is held by philology, and every other field builds on it: philosophy’s repairs need the object whole under one name, and reception’s alignments need it fixed. That is capital in the strict sense: a decision made once and drawn on by every field downstream.

It is kept at the gate. In Classical Philology’s first run, 1906–29, the nineteen names on its masthead held 27% of its bylines, the founding editor alone 109 items. Since 2010 nine authors in ten publish there once. The capital no longer accumulates in persons; it stays with the department that edits the journal, the Press that holds its copyright, and the anonymous reader of each decision.

How it is spent

The decision is spent as the received text. The items that decide what a text is are cited at 2.3 an item against 5.3 for the journal’s other work, a gap that holds within every period and every length band above two pages. The study states the mechanism as a hypothesis: once an emendation or an attribution is accepted, the reader works from the text it produced, and the argument that produced it is not what gets cited. The decision circulates; its reasons stay behind the gate. The publisher’s metadata deposit carries no abstract and no licence in 11,206 records, and 97% of the journal’s works are classed closed.

The article economy and the margin

Two further accounts close the circuit. In philosophy, the journal certifies the person through a unit that cannot fail, the repair of a received object, and the field takes the shape of what can be certified (#1773 §5). In reception, the widening moves semantic wealth inward: the margin supplies the receptions, and the centre keeps the object, the articles behind its subscription, the citations and the standing (#1770 §3.4.1). Both draw on the object philology fixed.

Inspectability against power

Across the three, openness runs against power. The journal with the least power over the object deposits abstracts and opens articles under Creative Commons; the journal with the most deposits no abstract, forbids posting before submission, assigns copyright to the university, and classes the use of AI in composition as research misconduct. The study of how it decides could not have been submitted to it. It is in the commons instead.

The case

The sharpest instance is one poem. What Sappho fragment 31 is, a woman undone by jealousy or a maker inscribing the reader who will sit opposite her voice, is decided where the text is decided; the jealousy reading was written into the text in 1816 and filed, never argued (ΦΑΙΝΕΤΑΙ ΜΟΙ, #1781). The remedy the census proposes is procedural: make the comparison inspectable, and the decision passes to whoever reads with the record in hand.
